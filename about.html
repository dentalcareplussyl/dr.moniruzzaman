THE FOUNDATIONS & EDUCATION

Establishing Clinical Benchmarks in Sylhet

A dedicated approach to oral healthcare requires integrating solid educational foundations with advanced clinical methods. Originally from Tangail, Dr. Md. Moniruzzaman Monir initiated his professional education with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from Dhaka University. To develop his technical expertise, he completed his Postgraduate Training (PGT) at Sir Salimullah Medical College & Hospital (SSMCH), followed immediately by a Masters of Public Health (MPH) to evaluate oral care through a broader biological approach.

His surgical values were shaped during his studies as part of the prestigious Batch-SDC-2 at Sapporo Dental College. This training developed his eye for detail and commitment to patient care. Outside the operatory, Dr. Moniruzzaman is an avid traveller, finding inspiration in exploring diverse global cultures and landscapes—a passion that informs his calm, patient-focused approach in the clinic.

Advanced Soft-Tissue Laser Therapy

Precision soft-tissue diode lasers allow for gum recontouring, biological sterilization, and cold-sore treatments without the need for surgical incisions or traditional sutures.

  • Clinical Target: Gumline rebalancing and gentle soft-tissue remodeling.
  • Materials Used: Non-contact diode laser wavelengths.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Non-contact pocket scanning & vascular evaluation.
02

Dental Implant Restorations

A dependable, long-term solution to replace missing teeth. This procedure places a sturdy medical-grade fixture directly into the jawbone, restoring full chewing capacity and natural appearance.

  • Clinical Target: Permanent, secure replacement for missing teeth to restore chewing and biting force.
  • Materials Used: Highly biocompatible titanium implant fixtures and custom dental crowns.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Standard radiographic bone evaluation and custom placement planning.
03

Porcelain Veneers & Smile Artistry

We craft microscopic feldspathic glass ceramic structures tailored to harmonize naturally with your specific facial contours. Our digital visualization workflows ensure minimum tooth structural modifications while restoring long-term functional balance.

  • Clinical Target: Structural alignment, discoloration, and structural adjustments.
  • Materials Used: Biocompatible lithium disilicate (E-Max) and feldspathic glass.
  • Diagnostic Steps: 3D Intraoral Scans & Virtual Smile Simulation setup.
04

Full-Arch Rehabilitation & Smile Makeovers

For extensive wear or complex structural collapse, we map complete facial proportions to plan full-arch biological ceramic restorations, bringing harmony back to your bite alignment.

  • Clinical Target: Rebuilding complete oral functions, height, and balanced aesthetics.
  • Materials Used: Micro-milled biological monolithic zirconia.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Digital Facebow Mapping & Multi-planar Occlusion Tracking.
05

Ultrasonic Root Canal Irrigation & Disinfection

Using advanced sonic and ultrasonic irrigation devices, we thoroughly sanitize internal root canal anatomy. This deep disinfection process ensures the complete elimination of bacteria in complex microscopic areas before filling.

  • Clinical Target: Eradicating hard-to-reach canal pathogens and preventing reinfection.
  • Materials Used: High-frequency ultrasonic activator tips and sterilized irrigating agents.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Multi-angle digital radiography and tactile canal exploration.
06

Chairside Teeth Whitening & Cosmetic Contouring

A fast, secure, and highly effective aesthetic procedure designed to brighten your teeth and reshape minor enamel imperfections. We use professional-grade formulations for immediate, natural-looking aesthetic results.

  • Clinical Target: Eliminating deep dental stains and reshaping asymmetrical tooth margins.
  • Materials Used: Advanced dental whitening gels and high-precision diamond finishing burs.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Shade guide calibration and aesthetic facial proportion mapping.
07

Atraumatic Wisdom Tooth Surgery

Specialized surgical extraction designed to remove impacted third molars with minimal discomfort. We utilize gentle techniques to protect the surrounding bone and speed up post-operative recovery.

  • Clinical Target: Safe removal of impacted wisdom teeth while preserving surrounding bone walls.
  • Materials Used: Specialized surgical instrumentation and comfortable localized anesthesia.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Panoramic digital radiography to assess nerve paths and root structure.
08

Periodontal Regeneration Therapy

Advanced clinical therapies utilizing non-surgical and localized techniques to restore damaged supportive bone and soft tissue structures compromised by advanced gum disease.

  • Clinical Target: Halting gum recession and regenerating supportive periodontal attachments.
  • Materials Used: Bioactive proteins, collagen membranes, and localized disinfecting agents.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Full-mouth periodontal probing depth mapping and bone volume analysis.
09

Fixed Orthodontic Treatment

Traditional fixed brackets and archwires to correct moderate-to-severe tooth misalignment, crowding, and malocclusion, guiding teeth into optimal positions over time.

  • Clinical Target: Correction of malocclusion, spacing, crowding, and bite misalignment.
  • Materials Used: Ceramic or high-grade stainless steel brackets and contouring wires.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Cephalometric analysis, panoramic radiography, and diagnostic study models.
10

Orthodontic Bracket & Retainer Bonding

High-precision adhesive techniques used to securely bond orthodontic brackets and fixed retainers to your teeth. This ensures your corrective or protective appliances remain perfectly stabilized throughout your treatment.

  • Clinical Target: Stable adhesion of corrective orthodontic appliances and long-term tooth retention.
  • Materials Used: Bio-friendly dental adhesives and light-cured composite cements.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Visual surface prep checking and anatomical positioning templates.
11

Periodontal Health & Bone Loss Assessment

Comprehensive evaluations designed to map and monitor the health of your supporting gum and bone tissues. This diagnostic service detects early structural changes to prevent tooth mobility.

  • Clinical Target: Early detection of localized bone loss and measuring gum pocket depths.
  • Materials Used: Calibrated periodontal probes and low-dose digital imaging.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Automated dental charting and bone density analysis.
12

Endodontic Assessment & Diagnostics

Highly accurate diagnostic screenings to evaluate the health of the internal dental pulp. We assess tooth vitality to plan the most conservative and effective treatments possible.

  • Clinical Target: Diagnosing internal pulp vitality, silent tooth infections, and hidden decay.
  • Materials Used: Electronic pulp testers and specialized thermal diagnostic tools.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Precise diagnostic trace mapping and multi-planar X-rays.
13

Adhesive Cementation of Crowns & Veneers

The technical process of permanently bonding customized crowns, veneers, or overlays onto natural teeth. We use premium resin-based cementation to ensure structural longevity and seamless margins.

  • Clinical Target: Permanent, secure structural sealing of dental prosthetics.
  • Materials Used: High-strength dual-cure resin cements and enamel prep solutions.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Fit verification under magnification and bite contact mapping.
14

Removable Alignment & Retention Therapy

Custom-designed removable appliances, including clear retainers and orthodontic plates, designed to hold teeth in position, correct minor shifts, or replace select teeth comfortably.

  • Clinical Target: Stabilizing tooth positions and correcting minor teeth spacing issues.
  • Materials Used: Lightweight, medical-grade clear thermoplastic or dental-grade acrylic plates.
  • Diagnostic Steps: High-accuracy dental impressions and custom model fabrication.
15

Precision Root Canal Therapy (RCT)

A safe, reliable procedure to eliminate dental pain and preserve your natural tooth. We perform thorough canal cleaning and shaping using standard, highly precise rotary instruments to ensure a comfortable and lasting result.

  • Clinical Target: Removing internal tooth infection and preserving native structures.
  • Materials Used: Biocompatible gutta-percha filling points and high-quality root canal sealers.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Precise digital dental X-rays to map root configurations.
16

Biocompatible Restorations (Composite Fillings)

We replace old fillings and repair structural decay using clean, mercury-free composite resins. These advanced materials bond directly to your natural teeth, ensuring safety, strength, and seamless blending.

  • Clinical Target: Safe metal filling replacement, structural support, and composite bonding.
  • Materials Used: Premium bisphenol-A (BPA) free hybrid composite polymers.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Digital decay transillumination and structural mapping.
17

Cosmetic Dental Bonding

A conservative aesthetic solution to repair minor dental damage, chips, or gaps. Tooth-colored composite resins are shaped directly on your teeth to immediately restore a balanced look.

  • Clinical Target: Instant repair of chips, minor cracks, and cosmetic gaps.
  • Materials Used: Highly polishable, shade-matched restorative micro-composites.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Shade matching and natural light reflection tests.
18

PFM Bridge Restorations

Durable Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) dental bridges that anchor onto adjacent teeth to replace missing gaps, restoring functional chewing surfaces and natural-looking tooth shapes.

  • Clinical Target: Bridging missing tooth gaps with high-durability restorations.
  • Materials Used: Dental porcelain layered over strong medical-grade metal alloys.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Digital diagnostic models and bite registration.
19

Complete & Partial Dentures

Tailored complete and partial prosthetic solutions designed to restore full chewing function and natural facial aesthetics for patients experiencing extensive or complete tooth loss. We focus on custom tissue adaptation, correct jaw alignment, and cosmetic shade selection.

  • Clinical Target: Full or partial arch tooth replacement, restoring vertical jaw support, and enhancing facial profiles.
  • Materials Used: High-impact medical-grade acrylic resins, hypoallergenic polymer bases, and custom-characterized aesthetic denture teeth.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Anatomical border molding, jaw relation registration, and personalized phonetic and aesthetic try-ins.
20

Atraumatic Tooth Extraction & Minor Surgery

Safe and gentle tooth removal using specialized instruments to preserve the surrounding bone structure and minimize tissue trauma, laying a foundation for successful future replacements.

  • Clinical Target: Gentle removal of non-restorable teeth while preserving healthy bone socket walls.
  • Materials Used: Localized anesthetics and biological socket preservation materials.
  • Diagnostic Steps: High-resolution digital dental X-rays to assess root anatomy.
21

Composite & Ceramic Restorative Polishing

A professional polishing treatment to restore the smooth surface and high shine of your existing crowns, veneers, or composite restorations, helping to prevent bacterial buildup and future stains.

  • Clinical Target: Maintenance of existing dental restorations to protect structural margins.
  • Materials Used: Fine diamond polishing pastes and specialized micro-abrasive disks.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Visual inspection under high illumination and surface texture check.
22

Restorative Cavity Preparation & Drilling

Careful and precise cleaning of decayed tooth structures prior to placing restorations. We focus on removing only damaged portions of the tooth to preserve maximum natural strength.

  • Clinical Target: Removing decay while protecting the vital nerve chamber underneath.
  • Materials Used: High-speed water-cooled dental instruments and decay-detecting dyes.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Magnified clinical visualization and mechanical cavity mapping.
23

Pit & Fissure Sealant Application

A preventive, non-invasive shield applied to the deep grooved biting surfaces of posterior teeth, stopping food particles and decay-causing bacteria from settling in.

  • Clinical Target: Long-term prevention of bite-surface dental decay.
  • Materials Used: Fluid tooth-colored biocompatible resin sealants.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Deep fissure cleaning evaluation and visual structural check.
24

Diagnostic Dental Radiography & Planning

High-resolution, low-exposure dental imaging used to safely inspect areas underneath your gums and between teeth, forming a complete blueprint for your safe treatment plan.

  • Clinical Target: Detecting hidden pathology, mapping bone density, and visual planning.
  • Materials Used: Digital intraoral sensors and diagnostic radiography equipment.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Full-mouth radiographic screening and multi-angle planning.
25

Routine Oral Examinations, Scaling & Polishing

Professional ultrasonic scaling and polishing designed to remove hardened plaque and tartar buildup, leaving your gums healthy, breath fresh, and teeth naturally bright.

  • Clinical Target: Prevention of gum disease, removing calculus, and fresh breath maintenance.
  • Materials Used: High-frequency ultrasonic scaler tips and gentle polishers.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Global oral health examinations and soft-tissue evaluation.
26

Emergency Trauma Management

Immediate, time-critical clinical care for acute oral injuries, including fractured, loose, or avulsed teeth, soft tissue lacerations, and sudden severe dental pain.

  • Clinical Target: Immediate stabilization of dental structures, pain relief, and tooth salvage.
  • Materials Used: Flexible splinting materials, bioactive pulp caps, and rapid-acting analgesics.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Urgent localized digital imaging and vitality assessments.
