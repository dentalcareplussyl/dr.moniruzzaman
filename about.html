THE FOUNDATIONS & EDUCATION

Establishing Clinical Benchmarks in Sylhet

A dedicated approach to oral healthcare requires integrating solid educational foundations with advanced clinical methods. Originally from Tangail, Dr. Md. Moniruzzaman Monir initiated his professional education with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from Dhaka University. To develop his technical expertise, he completed his Postgraduate Training (PGT) at Sir Salimullah Medical College & Hospital (SSMCH), followed immediately by a Masters of Public Health (MPH) to evaluate oral care through a broader biological approach.

His surgical values were shaped during his studies as part of the prestigious Batch-SDC-2 at Sapporo Dental College. This training developed his eye for detail and commitment to patient care. Outside the operatory, Dr. Moniruzzaman is an avid traveller, finding inspiration in exploring diverse global cultures and landscapes—a passion that informs his calm, patient-focused approach in the clinic.

CLINICAL SERVICES COMPREHENSIVE MATRIX

Specialized Care Programs

Explore our collection of clinical methodologies. Expand each program to view descriptive details, treatment summaries, and visual demos.

01

Porcelain Veneers & Smile Artistry

We craft microscopic feldspathic glass ceramic structures tailored to harmonize naturally with your specific facial contours. Our digital visualization workflows ensure minimum tooth structural modifications while restoring long-term functional balance.

  • Clinical Target: Structural alignment, discoloration, and structural adjustments.
  • Materials Used: Biocompatible lithium disilicate (E-Max) and feldspathic glass.
  • Diagnostic Steps: 3D Intraoral Scans & Virtual Smile Simulation setup.
02

3D Guided Dental Implants

Our surgical guide technologies integrate with high-resolution 3D CBCT scans to map physical bone density before treatment. Implants are positioned within ideal coordinates, ensuring optimal healing profiles.

  • Clinical Target: Permanent and biocompatible replacements for missing teeth.
  • Materials Used: Pure medical-grade Titanium or Zirconium fixtures.
  • Diagnostic Steps: 3D CBCT Bone Volume Analysis & Surgical Guide Planning.
03

Microscopic Root Canal Therapy

Working under high-magnification Carl Zeiss operative scopes, we isolate and disinfect anatomical canal structures, safely eliminating pulp infections while preserving deep tooth boundaries.

  • Clinical Target: Relieving localized infections while protecting native structures.
  • Materials Used: Thermally consolidated gutta-percha and bioceramic sealers.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Electronic Apex Locators & High-Magnification Microscopy.
04

Biological & Mercury-Free Restorations

We replace old fillings using mercury-safe clinical protocols. Our restorative resin inlays bond seamlessly to native tooth structures, eliminating micro-leaks and temperature sensitivity.

  • Clinical Target: Safe metal filling replacement and clean structural restoration.
  • Materials Used: Bisphenol-A (BPA) free hybrid composite polymers.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Digital decay transillumination and structural mapping.
05

Advanced Soft-Tissue Laser Therapy

Precision soft-tissue diode lasers allow for gum recontouring, biological sterilization, and cold-sore treatments without the need for surgical incisions or traditional sutures.

  • Clinical Target: Gumline rebalancing and gentle soft-tissue remodeling.
  • Materials Used: Non-contact diode laser wavelengths.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Non-contact pocket scanning & vascular evaluation.
06

Full-Arch Biomimetic Reconstruction

For extensive wear or complex structural collapse, we map complete facial proportions to plan full-arch biological ceramic restorations, bringing harmony back to your bite alignment.

  • Clinical Target: Rebuilding complete oral functions, height, and balanced aesthetics.
  • Materials Used: Micro-milled biological monolithic zirconia.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Digital Facebow Mapping & Multi-planar Occlusion Tracking.
07

Atraumatic Wisdom Surgery with PRF

We perform third molar removals utilizing localized bone-protection protocols, combined with Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) extracted from your own blood to promote rapid tissue healing.

  • Clinical Target: Extraction of impacted wisdom teeth with minimal bone friction.
  • Materials Used: Autologous PRF membranes and absorbable collagen.
  • Diagnostic Steps: 3D CBCT nerve tracking and surgical guide setups.
08

Computer-Aided Inlays & Onlays

We design custom porcelain fragments to restore individual structural defects in damaged teeth, creating strong, biocompatible alternatives to standard composite treatments.

  • Clinical Target: Permanent structural repairs for deep fractures and bite surfaces.
  • Materials Used: Biocompatible hybrid zirconia glass-ceramics.
  • Diagnostic Steps: High-precision digital scanning & virtual milling simulations.
09

Periodontal Cellular Regeneration

By using modern non-surgical laser procedures, we target and disinfect compromised pocket structures, restoring lost gum attachments and stabilizing support bone foundations.

  • Clinical Target: Treating deep gum recessions and reinforcing structural support.
  • Materials Used: Laser-activated sterile biomaterials and collagen grids.
  • Diagnostic Steps: Automated computerized mapping of periodontal metrics.
10

Digital Occlusion & TMJ Therapy

Using computerized bite sensors, we measure functional balance across your tooth arches, correcting occlusion discrepancies and helping resolve jaw-joint tension and TMJ issues.

  • Clinical Target: Eliminating TMJ discomfort and restoring balanced bite paths.
  • Materials Used: Customized hard acrylic stabilizing orthopedic splints.
  • Diagnostic Steps: T-Scan digital pressure maps & joint vibration evaluations.
